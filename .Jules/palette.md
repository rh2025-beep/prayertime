## 2024-09-03 - Custom Modal ARIA Attributes
**Learning:** In this application, modals and fullscreen views lack built-in screen reader accessibility. Adding `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and linking the title with `aria-labelledby` is critical for making these custom components navigable by assistive technologies.
**Action:** When implementing or modifying custom modals or fullscreen views, ensure proper ARIA dialog attributes and title linkages are applied to maintain an accessible experience.
