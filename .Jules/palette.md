## YYYY-MM-DD - [Missing ARIA Roles in Modals]
**Learning:** Modals and icon-only buttons in this application frequently lack proper ARIA attributes (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-label`), impacting screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** Always ensure dialogs and their controls are explicitly labeled and structured when modifying or creating new UI components.
